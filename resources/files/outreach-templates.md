# Application and follow-up messages

Replace every bracket with details that fit the role. Check the facts and follow any contact instructions in the listing.

## Application email

Subject: Application for [role] - [reference number]

Hello [name / hiring team],

I am applying for [role] at [company]. In [relevant example], I [what you did]. That work involved [skill or responsibility from the job description].

I have attached my resume in the requested format. [Optional: one relevant portfolio or repository link.]

I would be happy to discuss the role. Thank you for your time.

[Your name]

## Referral request

Hello [name],

I saw the [role / reference] opening at [company]. Its focus on [responsibility] interests me, and I have worked on [relevant example].

Would you be comfortable reviewing my fit or explaining how the referral process works? The opening is here: [official job link].

Thank you for considering it. I understand if you are not able to help.

[Your name]

## Follow-up

Subject: Following up - [role / reference]

Hello [name / hiring team],

I applied for [role] on [date]. Could you share any update on the next steps? I am still interested in the work on [specific part of the role].

Thank you for your time.

[Your name]

Use the employer's timeline if they give one. Otherwise, roughly seven to ten business days is a reasonable starting point for a check-in. Respect no-contact instructions and avoid repeated messages.

Use contact channels published for applicants. Check unexpected requests through the company's official website, and do not pay to obtain a job.
